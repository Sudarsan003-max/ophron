# SEO / AIEO / GEO IMPLEMENTATION BRIEF

## Search intent and page architecture

Use the homepage to explain the company; service pages to win transactional searches; industry pages to demonstrate relevance; guides to answer research queries; and project pages to establish evidence.

**Primary service clusters:** dishwashing manpower Singapore; kitchen stewarding services Singapore; office cleaning services Singapore; commercial cleaning company Singapore; kitchen deep cleaning Singapore; exhaust hood cleaning Singapore; facility services Singapore; event cleaning Singapore.

**Industry pages:** /industries/restaurants; /industries/hotels; /industries/commercial-properties; /industries/events; /industries/fb-groups. Create only where genuinely distinct service detail and examples exist.

## AI-answer readiness

On each important page: explain what OPHRON is in one direct paragraph; name the city served; state what the service includes; state who it serves; describe how to request a quote; give honest FAQs; and distinguish direct services from partner-coordinated work.

## Technical recommendations

* Use one descriptive H1 per page, distinct titles/meta descriptions, clean canonical URLs and crawlable HTML text.
* Add Organization / LocalBusiness structured data with verified business name, address, phone, business identifiers and sameAs links. Use Service structured data on actual service pages and BreadcrumbList for hierarchy.
* Add FAQ content for users, but do not expect FAQ rich results for an ordinary commercial site.
* Do not mark up reviews or testimonials that are not genuinely attributable and eligible under search guidelines.
* Add original project photos, useful captions, alt text, authorship, updated dates and links to primary Singapore regulatory sources where relevant.
* Connect related pages internally: homepage → services → individual service → relevant industry → relevant case study → enquiry.
* Measure enquiries, qualified leads, calls, WhatsApp clicks, quote requests and signed contracts—not rankings alone.

## Claims and compliance gate — mandatory before launch

The supplied source asserts **140+ active OPHRON contracts, 20+ years' experience, 31 named clients, 100% SLA, 99.8% uptime, 30%+ savings, specific testimonials, NEA licensing, bizSAFE Level 3, WSQ certification, IRATA credentials and a unified corporate relationship with GKT**. The PDF establishes that these statements appear in the existing copy; it does **not** independently prove them. Obtain documentary evidence, confirm the exact licensed/contracting entity, secure brand permissions and distinguish OPHRON results from partner history. Until then, keep unverified statistics and endorsements off public pages. Avoid guarantees of SFA inspection grades, pathogen elimination, response times or regulatory certification unless documented and legally appropriate.

**Conversion principle:** Sell the next low-friction step—an assessment or scoped conversation—not a vague “platform audit.” The promise is less operational hassle, clear responsibility and support fitted to the site. Scarcity, guarantees and social proof must be real.
